# Radha-Krishna Background Video Asset Guide

To display your animated video of Radha and Krishna in the background:

### 1. File Name & Location
Place your video file directly inside this directory:
```
public/videos/radha-krishna.mp4
```
*(Or `radha-krishna.webm`)*

### 2. Recommended Settings for Best Visual Harmony & Performance
- **Resolution**: 1080p (`1920x1080`) or 720p (`1280x720`).
- **Framerate**: 24fps or 30fps (smooth cinematic looping).
- **Audio**: Muted / No audio track (the website has its own integrated music player so the video stays silent).
- **File Size**: Between **3MB - 12MB** for fast instant loading on mobile and desktop without buffering.
- **Looping**: Choose a video with a seamless loop or smooth fade transition at the loop point.

### 3. Automatic Blending & Theming
The website automatically applies:
- Deep Midnight Obsidian vignette mask (`#08090e`).
- Soft ambient opacity (~30%) and contrast tuning to match the champagne gold and cashmere rose palette.
- Dynamic starfield stardust and shooting stars layered over the video for a 3D celestial effect.
